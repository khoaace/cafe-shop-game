# PHASE 07 — PROGRESSION, ACHIEVEMENTS & RETENTION

Continue improving the existing Phaser 3 coffee shop game.

Do not rewrite the existing project or break manual preparation interactions.

## GOAL

Build long-term progression that motivates players to keep improving their café and mastering drink preparation.

## DAILY CHALLENGES

Generate one optional challenge per day.

Examples:
- Serve 8 customers
- Achieve Combo x5
- Complete 3 Perfect Brews
- Finish a shift without losing customers
- Earn 100.000đ revenue
- Serve 4 cà phê sữa đá orders

Display:
- Challenge description
- Current progress
- Reward amount
- Completion animation

Reward players once per challenge.

## ACHIEVEMENTS

Create persistent achievements:

"Ly cà phê đầu tiên"
- Successfully serve the first drink.

"Phù thủy pha phin"
- Reach 10 Perfect Brews.

"Khách quen của phố"
- Serve 50 customers.

"Ông hoàng giờ cao điểm"
- Finish rush hour without losing a customer.

"Quán ruột 5 sao"
- Achieve a high daily rating.

"Đại gia vỉa hè"
- Accumulate 500.000đ in lifetime earnings.

Show animated achievement notifications.

Create a small achievement collection screen.

## RECIPE MASTERY

Track successful preparations for each drink.

Show a mastery level per recipe.

Example:
- Beginner
- Skilled
- Expert
- Master

Higher mastery can unlock:
- Cosmetic drink effects
- Recipe badges
- Small nonessential bonuses
- New decorative items

Do not automatically complete physical preparation actions because of mastery.

## DIFFICULTY PROGRESSION

Gradually increase complexity across days.

Early game:
- Slower customers
- Simple recipes
- Generous patience

Mid game:
- More simultaneous orders
- More ingredient combinations
- Rush-hour pressure
- VIP visits

Late game:
- Harder daily challenges
- Greater emphasis on multitasking
- More complex scheduling decisions

Avoid difficulty spikes.

## PLAYER STATISTICS

Track:
- Total served customers
- Lifetime revenue
- Total Perfect Brews
- Favorite recipe
- Best combo
- Best day
- Total play sessions

Display statistics in an attractive in-game notebook.

## BALANCE REVIEW

Review the game economy and pacing.

Identify and fix:
- Overly repetitive interactions
- Unfair waiting time
- Excessively difficult manual gestures
- Too many simultaneous customers
- Upgrades that are too expensive or weak
- Reward systems that trivialize gameplay

Manual coffee preparation must remain enjoyable throughout long-term progression.

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- Daily challenges work.
- Achievements unlock once.
- Progress saves correctly.
- Recipe mastery tracks correctly.
- Difficulty rises gradually.
- Rewards feel meaningful.
- Long-term progression does not undermine hands-on gameplay.

Focus on replayability, mastery and satisfaction rather than artificial grinding.
